Behind the build 🐶

Working notes on the original idea, prototype, assumptions and questions still to test.

01 · The starting hunch

Small dog daycares need a simpler day-to-day system

The original idea is that independent dog daycares could benefit from one straightforward place to manage bookings, keep dog parents updated and track the vaccination status of dogs attending daycare.

If it removes time spent juggling WhatsApp messages, diaries and paperwork, a business may be willing to pay a monthly subscription for it. This is a hypothesis to test, rather than a proven demand.

02 · Who it's for

Independent daycares, not big chains

The initial audience is smaller, owner-operated dog daycares in Ireland, particularly teams looking after around 20–50 dogs a day.

  • Booking requests arrive through different channels.
  • Staff need to know how many spaces are left each day.
  • Vaccination dates should be easy to check before check-in.
  • Dog parents appreciate a simple update about the dog's day.
03 · The proposed solution

One calm, practical daycare desk

The product concept brings together bookings and capacity, visible vaccination status, check-in and quick activity updates such as Ate, Walked, Played and Napped. An owner-facing update, called a “Pupdate,” is part of the longer-term vision.

The objective is to make everyday tasks quick enough for busy daycare staff to complete on a phone.

04 · Pricing assumption

Could daycares pay €50–€80 a month?

The original estimate was that businesses might pay roughly €50–€80 per month, possibly alongside a one-off joining or setup fee. The landing-page example uses €59 per month and a €149 setup fee.

Important: These are proposed prices only. No customer interviews, paid subscriptions or willingness-to-pay tests are being claimed.
05 · What the demo actually does

A working browser prototype, not a live service

  • Displays sample dog bookings and remaining daily places.
  • Flags vaccinations as in-date, expiring soon or expired.
  • Allows simulated check-ins and one-tap activity notes.
  • Shows a preview of an individual dog's update.

It uses invented data and saves changes in the current browser. It does not connect to real customer accounts, send owner notifications, collect secure medical documents or process payments. The landing-page waiting-list form is a demonstration and does not submit data.

06 · What to test next

Validate the problem before building further

  • Speak to independent dog daycare owners about how they currently book and check dogs in.
  • Find out where time is lost and which jobs cause the most mistakes.
  • Ask which features they would use every day and which are unnecessary.
  • Test whether €50–€80 monthly and a joining fee feel worthwhile.
  • Before storing real dog/owner records, plan privacy, security, permissions and data retention appropriately.
